# Glossary

An online newspaper for Santiago del Estero, Argentina. It is one publication, run by its own newsroom, written in Spanish and paid for by ads.

## People

**Reader** (_lector_):
Anyone who visits the public site. Readers are anonymous; they don't log in or have accounts.
_Avoid_: User, visitor, subscriber

**Staff** (_staff_ / _redacción_):
A person on the publication's team who logs in to produce or manage content. Only staff log in, and all staff have the same permissions.
_Avoid_: User, admin, member, editor, writer

## Content

**Post** (_nota_):
A single piece of news written by staff. A post is either a draft or published.
_Avoid_: Article, story, news item

**Draft** (_borrador_):
A post that is saved but not visible to readers.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, pending

**Published** (_publicada_):
A post that is visible to readers.
_Avoid_: Live, public, active

**Author** (_autor_):
The staff member who created a post.
_Avoid_: Byline, writer, creator

**Category** (_categoría_):
A topic that groups posts, such as Política, Deportes or Policiales. A post can belong to several categories.
_Avoid_: Section, tag, topic

## Advertising

**Ad** (_publicidad_):
A banner (an image with a link) that the publication sold to a business and that staff manage. An ad is either active or inactive.
_Avoid_: Advert, banner, sponsor, campaign

**Ad slot** (_espacio publicitario_):
A fixed place on the site's pages where an ad can appear.
_Avoid_: Placement, zone, position
